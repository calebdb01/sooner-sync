Auto sync

The radio goes silent while the phone listens to the TV (20 to 60 seconds). It matches the stadium sound that the TV and the radio share.

Tips for the best auto sync
  • Let the radio play for 2 minutes before you start auto sync. The app can only match radio that it has saved.
  • Start auto sync during live play. Do not start it during ads, replays or halftime.
  • Turn the TV volume up. Hold the phone 1 to 3 feet from the TV speaker.
  • Keep the room quiet while the phone listens.
  • Do not use Bluetooth earphones during auto sync.
  • Keep this page open and the screen on. Set the ring switch to ring, not silent.
  • If auto sync is not sure, use Pause and Resume. Then adjust with the 0.2 buttons.
  • If the radio is behind the TV, pause the TV for some seconds. Then sync again.

Some games are blocked on the web stream for rights reasons. If you hear normal shows and not the game, use a different stream link.
