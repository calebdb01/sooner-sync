      Radio delay
      –

      Sooner makes the radio play earlier. Later makes it play later. Hold a button to move faster.

      If the radio is behind the TV, pause the TV for a few seconds, then line them up again.

      How to use
      • Tap Play. The radio starts 5 seconds behind live.
      • Tap Pause when the radio calls the snap.
      • Tap Resume when the TV shows that same snap.
      • Use Sooner or Later to fix a small error. Check again on the next play.
      • Turn the TV sound down. Keep this page open.
