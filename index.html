How to use
  • Manual sync: Tap Pause when the radio calls the snap. Tap Resume when the TV shows that snap. Then use Sooner or Later for small errors.
  • Auto sync: The radio goes silent for 20 to 45 seconds while the phone listens to the TV.
  • Start auto sync during live play. Do not start it during ads, replays or halftime.
  • Turn the TV volume up. Hold the phone 1 to 3 feet from the TV speaker. Keep the room quiet.
  • Do not use Bluetooth earphones during auto sync.
  • If the radio is behind the TV, pause the TV for some seconds. Then sync again.
