0.0 s
Radio is ahead of TV. This adds a delay to the radio.

Listens to the TV for 30 seconds and matches crowd noise, band and whistles to the radio. Best during live play, not during ads. Turn TV sound up.

Or tap sync

Tap 1 when you hear the play on radio. Tap 2 when you see it on TV.

Fine tune

If the radio is behind the TV, pause the TV (DVR) for that many seconds, then sync again. Some games may be blocked on the web stream for rights reasons. If you hear normal shows instead of the game, use a different stream link.
